Area

Lake Balaton, affectionately known as the "Hungarian Sea," is the largest freshwater lake in Central Europe. Located just 100 km southwest of Budapest, this 77-kilometre-long expanse is Hungary's ultimate summer playground. It offers a beautiful mix of shallow beaches, volcanic wine regions, historic towns, and thermal spas.

Things to do

Around Lake Balaton, you can craft the perfect mix of relaxation, outdoor activity, and culture depending on which shore you visit:

• Relax on the Water: Swim in the shallow, warm waters of the Southern Shore (ideal for families), or rent a sailboat or stand-up paddleboard from Balatonfüred to get out on the open lake.

• Go Wine Tasting: Hike up the volcanic hills of Badacsony or the Csopak region to tour traditional cellars and sip local white wines like Olaszrizling with panoramic lake views.

• Explore History & Culture: Visit the stunning 11th-century Benedictine Abbey on the Tihany Peninsula, stroll through the grand rooms of the baroque Festetics Palace in Keszthely, or walk the historic Tagore Promenade.

• Unwind in Thermal Waters: Take a short side-trip to Lake Hévíz at the western tip to float among water lilies in the world's second-largest natural thermal lake.

• Get Active: Cycle a portion of the Balaton Bike Ring, a 210 km dedicated path that circles the entire lake, or hike through the unique basalt rock formations in the Balaton Uplands National Park.
